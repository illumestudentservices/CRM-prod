# Collecting Leads at Events

## Standard Operating Procedure

**Illume Student Advisory Services — International Client Representatives**

---

This is how to collect student leads at a fair or school visit when there is no
internet, and get them into the CRM afterwards.

Everything is done from **Students & Pipeline → Offline Capture**.

---

## Before you travel

**Do this while you still have wifi — it will not work at the venue.**

1. Sign in to the CRM on the phone or laptop you will use at the event.
2. Go to **Students & Pipeline**, then tap **Offline Capture**.
3. Tap **Prepare for offline**.

![Leads page](images/03-leads-banner.png)

You will see a warning until this is done:

![Not prepared](images/04-capture-empty.png)

Once prepared, the date it was downloaded is shown:

![Prepared](images/05-prepared.png)

**Do this on the day you travel.** If the lists are weeks old, an event or agent
added since then will not appear, and you will not be able to record it.

> **If you skip this step, Lead Source will be empty at the booth.** Lead Source
> is required before a student can be moved past "New Lead", so those leads
> would be stuck and need re-entering by hand.

---

## At the booth

### Recording a student

Fill in the form and tap **Save to device**.

![Form filled in](images/06-form-filled.png)

**You must have both an email address and a phone number.** Ask for both while
the student is still in front of you — the form will not save without them.

### If the event uses scannable badges

Tap **Scan badge** and hold the badge inside the frame. Whatever can be read is
shown for you to check before it fills the form, and you can change anything.

Badges differ between organisers. Some carry a full contact card, others only an
attendee number — so always check what came through.

> **Scan badge only appears on Android phones using Chrome.** iPhones cannot do
> this. Type the details in as normal.

### Permission to email

Every student must be asked: **"May we email them?"**

Canadian anti-spam law requires their permission before we send marketing email.

| Answer | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Yes, they agreed** | We may email them |
| **No, they declined** | We must not email them |
| **Didn't ask** | Nobody asked — we can still ask later |

**"Didn't ask" is not the same as "declined."** Record it honestly. Marking
someone as declined when they were never asked means we lose a student we could
have contacted.

## Working with no internet

Everything works exactly the same. The badge at the top changes to
**No connection**, and **Upload** is greyed out until you are back online.

![Offline](images/08-offline.png)

Carry on collecting. The number beside **Upload** shows how many are waiting.

![Two held](images/09-offline-queue.png)

**Maximum 100 students per session.** At 100 the form stops accepting new ones
until you upload.

---

## Uploading

When you are back on wifi, the badge returns to **Connected**.

![Back online](images/10-back-online.png)

Tap **Upload**. The device clears and the students are in the CRM.

![Uploaded](images/11-uploaded.png)

**Upload at the end of every day.** Until you do, those students exist only on
that one device. If it is lost, broken or wiped, they are gone.

---

## If something goes wrong

**The upload stopped halfway.**
Tap **Upload** again. Students who already went through are recognised and will
not be added twice.

**A student was rejected.**
They stay on the device with a note explaining what is wrong. Tap the pencil
next to them, correct it, and save. They will go up with the next upload. Other
students are unaffected.

**It says I have been signed out.**
Sign in again and tap **Upload**. Nothing has been lost.

**The upload failed completely.**
Nothing is removed from the device when an upload fails. Try again when you have
a better connection.

**Nothing appears when I open the page without internet.**
The page must have been opened at least once while connected. Reconnect, open
Offline Capture once, and it will work without a signal from then on.

---

## Rules to follow

1. **Prepare for offline on the day you travel** — not the week before.
2. **Always collect both an email and a phone number.**
3. **Always ask about email permission** and record the honest answer.
4. **Never invent details** to get past the form. A made-up email address is
   worse than no lead at all — it cannot be contacted, and it corrupts our
   records.
5. **Upload at the end of every day.**
6. **Do not use a private or incognito window.** Leads cannot be saved in one.
7. **Use your own device** where possible. Leads are filed under whoever uploads
   them, so uploading a colleague's work records it as yours.
