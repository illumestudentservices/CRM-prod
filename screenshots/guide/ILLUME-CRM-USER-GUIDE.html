1. Dashboard

The Dashboard is your landing page after login. It shows a high-level snapshot of the entire CRM.

Dashboard - Overview with key metrics, pipeline stats, and recent activity

What You See

  • Total Leads, Enrolled, Conversion Rate - Pipeline metrics at a glance

2. Client Management (Institutions)

The Clients module manages your institutional partnerships - universities, colleges, and other education providers. This is the backbone of the CRM.

2.1 Client List

Navigate: Sidebar > Clients

Clients page - Card view showing 9 partner institutions with lead counts, ICR assignments, and contract counts
  • Summary cards at top: Total Partners (9), Active (7), Renewal Due (0), Prospects (2)
  • Search & filter: by name, status, type, or region
  • Each card shows leads count, ICRs assigned, contracts count
  • Click any card to open the institution detail page
  • + Add Institution button (top-right) to create a new client

2.2 Client Detail Page

Click any institution card to see its full profile.

UCL detail page - Shows status, contract value ($15,000), renewal date, overview, and Governance dashboard with KPI progress bars

The detail page has 8 tabs:

Governance

Pipeline stats, budget usage, KPI achievement bars, recent activities, open risks & compliance items.

Overview

Strategic objectives, enrollment targets vs actuals, yearly performance charts.

Team

Account manager, assigned ICRs and their contact details.

Contacts

Institution contacts (Director of Recruitment, Regional Manager, etc.).

Contracts

Partnership agreements with dates, values, renewal warnings, and file attachments.

Engagement

History of meetings, calls, and emails with the institution.

KPIs

Tracked KPIs by category with progress bars. Filter by year.

Documents

Uploaded files related to the institution.

2.3 KPIs Tab

KPIs tab for UCL (2025) - 6 KPIs tracked across Recruitment, Engagement, Market Development, and Relationship categories
  • Year selector at top to switch between years
  • KPIs grouped by category (Recruitment, Engagement, Market Development, Relationship)
  • Each KPI shows: name, period (Annual/Quarterly), current vs target, progress bar, percentage
  • "Update Progress" link to update current values
  • "+ Add KPI" button to create new KPIs

2.4 Contracts Tab (with Attachments)

Contracts tab - Table shows title, value, dates, status, renewal warnings, and a Files column with attachment count
  • Each contract row has a chevron (>) - click to expand and see/upload attachments
  • Files column shows attachment count with paperclip icon
  • When expanded: "Attach File" button to upload documents (PDF, DOCX, images, up to 5MB)
  • Attachments can be downloaded (click download icon) or deleted (hover to see trash icon)
  • Renewal warnings show as colored badges: red for expired or < 30 days, amber for < 60 days

3. Students & Pipeline

Navigate: Sidebar > Students & Pipeline

Student pipeline - 53 leads across 9 stages with filters, search, and stage-based color coding

How It Works

  1. Lead Created - Student enters as NEW (via form, agent referral, or event)
  2. CONTACTED - ICR makes first contact
  3. APPLICATION_SENT - Application submitted to institution
  4. DOCUMENTS_RECEIVED - Supporting documents collected
  5. OFFER_ISSUED - Institution issues an offer letter
  6. ENROLLED - Student accepts and pays deposit
  • Filter by: stage, institution, source, region, assigned ICR
  • Click any student to see full profile: documents, activity timeline, notes
  • Leads can also be DEFERRED, REJECTED, or LOST
  • The system tracks last contacted and last progressed dates for follow-up alerts

6. Activity Management

Navigate: Sidebar > Activities

Every field interaction is logged here: school visits, agent meetings, student events, fairs, and partner meetings.

Activities page - 6 logged activities with type, date, location, leads generated, cost, and linked entities

Activity Types

SCHOOL_VISIT

Presentations at feeder schools. Tracks students engaged, counsellors met, leads generated.

AGENT_MEETING

Quarterly reviews, training sessions with agent partners. Tracks ROI.

STUDENT_EVENT

Open days, virtual events, webinars. Tracks attendees and applications.

FAIR

Education fairs and exhibitions. Large-scale lead generation events.

PARTNER_MEETING

Strategic meetings with institutional partners. Discusses targets and new programs.

Logging an Activity

Log Activity dialog - Select type, fill in details, link to institution/market/school/agent, record outcomes
  • Click "+ Log Activity" to open the dialog
  • Select activity type - the form adapts based on type selected
  • Link to institution, market, school, or agent as relevant
  • Record: outcomes, follow-up actions, topics discussed, cost, ROI
  • Activities can auto-generate follow-up tasks based on action items

7. Tasks & Accountability

Navigate: Sidebar > Tasks

Tasks page - 8 tasks with priority badges, status tracking, assignees, due dates, and linked source activities

Key Features

  • Priority levels: Urgent (red), High (orange), Medium (blue), Low (gray)
  • Status workflow: To Do > In Progress > Done (or Cancelled)
  • Activity-linked toggle: Filter to see only tasks that came from activities
  • Source Activity column: Shows which activity generated this task (clickable link)
  • Assignee: Who is responsible for completing the task
  • Filter by: status, priority, activity-linked
  • "+ Create Task" to manually create tasks
Tip: When you log an activity with action items, the system can automatically create tasks assigned to the appropriate team member.

8. Travel Management

Navigate: Sidebar > Travel

Travel Management - 4 trips totaling $14,550 with status tracking (Pending, Approved, Completed)

Two Tabs

  • Travel Plans: List of all travel requests with destination, purpose, dates, estimated/actual costs, status
  • Travel Reporting: Aggregated travel analytics and expense summaries
Travel Reporting tab - Analytics on travel spending patterns

Travel Request Workflow

  1. Create - Employee creates travel request with destination, purpose, dates, estimated cost
  2. Pending - Request awaits manager approval
  3. Approved - Manager approves, travel can proceed
  4. Completed - After return, actual cost is recorded

Each travel request can have:

  • Itinerary items: flights, hotels, transfers with confirmation references
  • Meetings: scheduled meetings during the trip, linked to activities

9. Reporting Engine

Navigate: Sidebar > Reports

Reports page - Monthly reports with approval status workflow (Draft > Pending Review > Approved)

Report Types

  • Monthly Reports: ICRs submit monthly activity reports for each institution they manage. Includes leads data, program breakdown, source performance, KPI summary, engagement notes, challenges, and next month plan.
  • Weekly Activities: Quick weekly activity tracking (agent visits, school engagements, enquiries)
  • Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs): Comprehensive quarterly analysis per institution

Quarterly Business Reviews

QBR page - Quarterly Business Reviews with executive summaries, market performance, ROI analysis, and strategic recommendations
  • QBRs contain: executive summary, market performance breakdown, ROI analysis, strategic recommendations, KPI summary
  • Status: DRAFT > SUBMITTED > APPROVED
  • Can be generated and exported as PDF

Report Approval Workflow

  1. ICR creates monthly report (DRAFT)
  2. ICR submits for review (PENDING_REVIEW)
  3. Regional Manager reviews and approves (REGIONAL_APPROVED)
  4. HQ reviews and gives final approval (FINAL_APPROVED) or returns for edits

10. Risk & Compliance

Navigate: Sidebar > Risk & Compliance

10.1 Risk Register

Risk Register - 5 risks with type (Market/Client/Staff/Operational), likelihood, impact, risk score, status, and owner
  • Risk Types: MARKET, CLIENT, STAFF, OPERATIONAL
  • Risk Score = Likelihood (1-5) x Impact (1-5). Scores 15+ are critical.
  • Status: OPEN, ESCALATED, MITIGATED, CLOSED
  • Each risk has an owner, optional institution/market link, and mitigation plan
  • Color-coded risk scores: red (15+), orange (10-14), green (<10)

10.2 Compliance Tracker

Compliance Tracker - 6 items across GDPR, FOIPOP, CASL, Agent Compliance, and Training categories
  • Compliance Types: GDPR, FOIPOP, CASL, AGENT_COMPLIANCE, TRAINING, OTHER
  • Tracks: title, description, due date, assigned person, status (Pending/In Progress/Completed)
  • Summary cards show: Open Risks, Critical Risks, Pending Compliance, Overdue Compliance

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

The system uses a sophisticated RBAC model with 8 roles across 20+ resources and 5 actions (view, create, edit, delete, export).

  • SUPER_ADMIN: Full access to everything
  • HQ_EXECUTIVE: Read access to all modules, write access to reports and strategic items
  • HQ_ANALYTICS: Read-only access to analytics, reports, and pipeline data
  • REGIONAL_MANAGER: Full access to their region's data, approve reports
  • ICR: Manage their assigned institutions, students, activities; submit reports
  • INSTITUTION_CLIENT: View their own institution data only
  • HR_MANAGER: Full access to HR module (employees, leave, attendance, KB)
  • EMPLOYEE: View own data, submit leave requests, view announcements
Security Note: Permission overrides can be configured at Settings > Permissions to grant or revoke specific permissions per role. The system supports database-level permission overrides on top of the static RBAC matrix.

Sidebar Collapse

Click "Collapse" at the bottom of the sidebar to minimize it to icon-only mode. Hover over icons to see tooltips. Click again to expand.
